# VOR-069 historical backtest evidence boundary

The VOR-069 demonstration backtest is version-pinned to `vor069-historical-backtest-v1` and explicitly tagged `synthetic_demo`.

It may demonstrate that timestamped Vorta risk or a critical stock-out preceded a controlled historical outcome and, where linked reservation, non-reversed 261 goods movement and repair timestamps exist, that material availability constrained recovery.

It must not claim that timing proves breakdown causation or that an intervention would have prevented a later failure. False positives and successful interventions are retained as first-class validation cases.

The synthetic demonstration panel is rendered only in configured demo mode. Live equipment-history routes remain subject to the existing live-evidence trust boundary.
